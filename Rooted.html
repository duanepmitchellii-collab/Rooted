60-Second Morning Script
Today I do not need to prove anything. I need to be steady. My job is to lower pressure and raise safety. I will listen before I explain. I will follow through without being reminded. I will notice what needs doing and handle it. I can want closeness without chasing it. I can feel anxiety without obeying it. Today I will act like the husband and father I want to be — calm, present, accountable, and grounded.
2-Minute Grounding
1Feet on the floor. Unclench jaw and shoulders.
2Breathe in 4, hold 4, out 6–8. Repeat 5 times.
3Name 3 facts: “I am here. I am safe. I do not need to solve everything this morning.”
4Choose your posture: calm, warm, not chasing, not withdrawing.
